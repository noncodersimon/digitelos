Website privacy

Clear about your information.

This notice covers the Digitelos company website. Individual software products may have their own privacy notices.

Browser storage and enquiries

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this site. Your light-or-dark theme preference is stored in your browser and is not sent to us.

The contact form opens your own email app with a draft for you to review and send. The website does not submit or store the details you enter.

Email

If you email Digitelos, we use your message to respond to your enquiry and manage any work that follows. We retain correspondence for that purpose and do not sell it or add you to a marketing list.

Hosting and fonts

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical information such as IP addresses to operate the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's servers to request them, which shares technical information such as your IP address. See Google Fonts' privacy information.

Questions

For questions about this website or your correspondence, contact info@digitelos.co.uk.
