Slapton Village Hall - pickleball scheduler

Village hall · Slapton, Devon

The Slapton village hall had no web site, and bookings were managed on paper. The hall's website came with an explicit design brief - its colour scheme follows a painting that hangs in the hall itself.

Weekly pickleball sessions ended up with much confusion about how many games each person had played, and the rotation debates were becoming a fixture of their own.

A live rotation scheduler: fair turn-taking, shared live state, and it works on any phone brought to the hall.

Used every week. No more paper.

Pickle Rota

Digitelos project · free web app

The village hall scheduler above grew into something any one-court pickleball group can use.

Start a group with a name and an organiser password and you get two links: one to run the games, one for players to watch along on their phones. Everyone plays the same number of games, partners keep changing, and late arrivals join at the group's pace. Mixed pairs and men's and women's modes are built in, and control passes cleanly from one organiser to the next.

Free to use, no accounts, works on any phone.

pickle-rota.digitelos.co.uk
